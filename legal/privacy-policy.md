## 8. Data Retention

We retain personal data for the longer of:

- The duration of our relationship with you

- The retention period required by applicable law (typically 5-7 years following relationship termination for AML/CFT records under MAS requirements)

- The period required to defend potential claims (typically 6 years following the relevant transaction)

Following the retention period, personal data is deleted or anonymized.

## 9. On-Chain Data — Important Notice

Smart contract transactions executed in connection with the Services — including subscriptions, transfers, and yield claims — are recorded on public blockchains (Ethereum and supported networks). Such on-chain data is:

- Permanent and immutable by design

- Pseudonymous (associated with wallet addresses, not directly with personal identity)

- Available to anyone who queries the relevant blockchain

OmniFlow maintains the link between wallet addresses and personal identity in its internal systems (subject to access controls and the retention period above), but the on-chain records themselves cannot be deleted, modified, or anonymized once recorded.

By using the Services, you acknowledge and accept that on-chain data associated with your wallet is not subject to deletion, anonymization, or "right to be forgotten" requests, regardless of any rights you may have under applicable data protection law in respect of OmniFlow's internal records.
