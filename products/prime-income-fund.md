# Korea Prime Income Fund (Tier 1)

Stable income from prime Korean commercial real estate. Target net yield of 5–7% per annum.

## What It Is

The Korea Prime Income Fund is OmniFlow's lowest-risk, income-focused product. It invests in stabilized, income-producing prime real estate in Seoul's primary office submarkets (CBD, GBD, YBD), large-scale logistics centers in the Seoul metropolitan area, and ABL-secured fixed-income notes backed by prime commercial assets.

## Strategy

The fund acquires direct equity positions or senior secured debt positions in assets with established tenants, long-duration leases, and verified rent rolls. Asset selection prioritizes properties with weighted average lease terms (WALT) of three years or more, occupancy rates above 95%, and credit-tenant rosters.

The fund typically operates with no leverage or with conservative senior leverage at LTV ratios of 30–50%. Yield is generated primarily from rental income and, where applicable, secured note coupons.

## Risks

Tier 1 is the lowest-risk product in the OmniFlow line, but is not risk-free. Principal risks include:

- **Tenant credit risk** — vacancies or tenant defaults reduce rental income

- **Real estate market risk** — declines in Korean commercial property values affect NAV

- **Currency risk** — KRW/USD movements affect distribution amounts in stablecoin terms (partially hedged for large deals via NDF)

- **Liquidity risk** — secondary market depth may be limited; maturity redemption is the primary expected exit

- **Smart contract risk** — see Trust & Security

A complete risk disclosure is provided in the product Investment Memorandum (IM) issued at subscription.
