# Auditor Reports

This page lists the independent audits and attestations performed on OmniFlow's operations, fund accounts, and reserves. Reports are published in full and preserved in perpetuity.

## Audit Scope

OmniFlow is audited at multiple layers, with each audit performed by an independent firm:

| **Audit Layer** | **Scope** | **Auditor** | **Frequency** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Smart Contract Security | Production smart contracts and on-chain logic | Trail of Bits, OpenZeppelin (or equivalent) | Pre-deployment + post-modification |
| Formal Verification | Critical invariants (supply, gating, lockup) | Certora (or equivalent) | Pre-deployment |
| VCC Annual Audit | VCC sub-fund financial statements, NAV calculation, distribution accuracy | Big 4 firm | Annual |
| Korean REF Annual Audit | Korean REF financial statements per Korean accounting standards | Big 4 Korea or local equivalent | Annual |
| Reserve Attestation | On-chain supply vs. VCC sub-register reconciliation | Big 4 firm (verification component) | Quarterly |
| AML/CFT Program Review | Independent review of AML/CFT program effectiveness | [TBD — AML compliance specialist firm] | Annual |
| SOC 2 Type II | Operational, security, and availability controls | [TBD — SOC 2 audit firm] | Phase 2+ |

## Auditor Independence

OmniFlow follows the standard auditor independence practices:

- No fee arrangements that compromise auditor objectivity

- No non-audit services from the same firm in the audit period (per Big 4 firm independence policies)

- Auditor rotation considered at minimum every 5 years per institutional best practice

- Audit committee oversight at the OmniFlow Foundation level (Phase 2+)

## Disclosure Standard

All audit reports are published in full on this page in PDF format. Excerpts or summaries are not used as a substitute for full report disclosure. Where an auditor's professional standards limit certain disclosures, the limitations are explicitly noted.

Material adverse audit findings, including any qualified opinion or disclaimer of opinion, are disclosed within 5 business days of issuance through:

- This page

- The investor dashboard (institutional users)

- Direct relationship manager communication for material allocators
