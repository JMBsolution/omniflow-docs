# Auditor Reports

No audit or independent attestation has been performed on OmniFlow. There are no reports to publish on this page.

## Current Status

OmniFlow is a pre-funding company running a testnet demonstration on Base Sepolia. There is no fund vehicle, no fund accounts, no assets and no reserves, so most audit layers have no subject matter yet.

| **Audit or review** | **Status** |
| --- | --- |
| Smart contract security audit | Not performed. The deployed contracts are unaudited. |
| Formal verification | Not performed. |
| Financial statement audit | Not applicable — no fund vehicle and no fund accounts exist. |
| Reserve attestation | Not applicable — no reserves exist. |
| AML/CFT program review | Not performed. |
| SOC 2 Type II | Not performed. |

No audit, verification or attestation firm has been engaged or appointed for any of the above, and no engagement is under way.

## Unaudited Contracts

The contracts exercised in the demonstration are deployed to Base Sepolia testnet and have not been audited or formally verified by any third party. They should be read as a working demonstration, not as production-hardened code.

There is no bug bounty programme.

## Before Mainnet

An independent smart contract security audit is a precondition for any mainnet deployment. Financial statement audits and reserve attestations only become meaningful once a fund vehicle exists and holds assets on behalf of investors.

Reports will be published on this page when they exist.
